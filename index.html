1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, email, location, contacts, messages, photos library, device identifiers, payment details or browsing history. The tool does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or SMS.

2. Data saved on your device (local storage)

With your consent, the tool saves the following in the app’s or browser’s local storage, on your own phone or computer only:

  • your settings and choices: theme, fonts, accent colour, sheet and layout, sizes, watermark options and similar;
  • print lists you save, with the list names you give them, including names, prices, codes and small images inside them;
  • the optional side image and founder photo;
  • a record that you accepted the Terms, with the date, and your guide language.

This data is not uploaded to us and we cannot see it. Anyone who can use your device profile may see it, so do not keep private information on a shared device. Storage is limited (usually about 5 MB), so very large image lists may not save.

3. Access to your files

The tool opens a file only after you choose it in your device’s file picker (for example an image, logo or a marketplace label PDF). It cannot browse your device. Selected files are processed on your device and are not uploaded. You can refuse file access in the first-run popup and still make typed barcode, QR and text labels.

4. Third-party services

  • Fonts and PDF reader: the tool may load Google Fonts and the PDF reader library from Cloudflare cdnjs. Those providers can see technical data such as your IP address under their own policies.
  • Voice guide: the spoken guide uses your phone’s text-to-speech. Depending on your device and the voice installed, the device vendor may process the guide text online to produce speech. We receive none of it.
  • Store and hosting: app stores (such as Indus Appstore) and web hosts may keep their own download or server logs under their own policies.

5. How we use information

Only to run the tool, remember your settings and saved lists on your device, and record that you accepted the Terms. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or sale.

6. Your rights and deleting data

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side. You can delete everything on your device at any time with Settings → Erase all saved data, delete a single saved list from the list bar, withdraw consent in the Terms & Privacy tab, or clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. You may also write to us about access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent or any complaint (see section 10), as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Children

The tool is meant for sellers, students and makers aged 18 and above, or younger users under a parent’s or guardian’s supervision. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

Because processing happens on your device, your content does not travel to us. No system is completely secure, so keep your device and browser updated and do not store sensitive information in saved lists.
